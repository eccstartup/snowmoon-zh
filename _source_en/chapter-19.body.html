"Level two obfuscation is actually the right tradeoff for language models, because language models themselves are big and compute-intensive, so they can't handle the high overhead of going level four, and on the other hand, language models are at least partially obfuscated and resistant to bit flips all on their own. If you uncover the value of a few weights, or get the ability to reliably flip a few values in a few tensors, it's not like you can get anything useful out of that."
